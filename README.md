# BCAN Project: Building Carbon Emissions Analysisd

## 📋 Project Overview

This project was conducted in collaboration with the **Boston Climate Action Network (BCAN)** to analyze greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions from buildings in Boston under the *Building Emissions Reduction and Disclosure Ordinance (BERDO)**.

The goal was to evaluate compliance with BERDO standards, identify emission hotspots, and provide actionable policy recommendations to reduce Boston’s carbon footprint.

- **Focus:** BERDO compliance, GHG emissions analysis, and policy recommendations


The project was divided into key phases:


1. **Data Collection & Quality Assessment**


2. **GHG Emissions Analysis & Visualization**


3. **Policy Recommendations Based on Insights**


4. **Dashboard Development for Stakeholder Use**


---

## ⚙️ Technologies Used

- **Data Visualization:** Tableau


- **Data Processing:** Python, Excel


- **Mapping & GIS:** Geospatial analysis using Tableau maps

  
- **Data Sources:** BERDO Database, Tax Assessor’s Database, Income by Zip Code


---

## 🚀 Methodology

### 1. **Data Preparation**

- Integrated multiple datasets from Boston’s city databases.


- Conducted data cleaning to address missing values, inconsistencies, and standardization issues.


- Validated fields like `TaxParcelID` for improved data accuracy.


### 2. **GHG Emissions Analysis**

- Analyzed emissions by **building type**, **age**, and **location**.


- Identified emission hotspots, especially in older institutional and industrial buildings.


- Examined correlations between energy consumption, building characteristics, and GHG emissions.


### 3. **Compliance Analysis**

- Evaluated BERDO compliance across residential, commercial, and institutional buildings.


- Explored the impact of **Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs)** on compliance rates.


- Assessed the variation in compliance based on **socioeconomic factors** like income levels.


### 4. **Dashboard Development**

- Created an interactive Tableau dashboard for stakeholders to:


  - Filter data by compliance status, property type, and neighborhood.
  

  - Visualize emissions trends over time.
  

  - Identify properties needing targeted interventions.


---

## 📈 Results & Key Findings

- **High-Emission Hotspots:** Concentrated in older industrial zones and large institutional buildings.


- **PPA Participation:** Properties enrolled in PPAs showed higher compliance rates with BERDO.


- **Energy Inefficiencies:** Buildings with low Energy Star scores are critical targets for retrofitting.


- **Socioeconomic Disparities:** Non-compliance is more prevalent in lower-income neighborhoods.


---

## ✅ Insights & Recommendations

- **Target High-Emission Buildings:** Prioritize retrofitting programs for industrial and healthcare facilities.


- **Promote Renewable Energy:** Expand incentives for PPAs and other renewable energy initiatives.


- **Improve Data Quality:** Continue enhancing the BERDO database for better policy-making.


- **Equity-Focused Policies:** Address socioeconomic barriers to improve compliance in underserved communities.

---

## 🎯 Conclusion

This project provided critical insights into Boston’s building emissions landscape, highlighting key areas for intervention to achieve the city’s **net-zero emissions goal by 2050**.

By leveraging data-driven analysis and interactive dashboards, stakeholders can make informed decisions to promote climate justice and sustainable urban development.
